Design

Why the test was built this way

Each choice below answers one question a careful reader would ask.

Where the workflow comes from

The drug experiments were carried out with the open-source workflow located at:
https://anonymousauthorsdoubleblind.github.io/opensource-anonymous-contribution-535-workflow-paper-2026/index.html

The group first validated the open-source modules on annulenes, and then ran the same modules, unchanged, on the drug. The methods and the annulene runs are open-sourced at the address above.

The choices, one by one

Why 5-fluorouracil?

It is a widely used anticancer drug, small enough (12 atoms) for a full Hessian on one cloud GPU, and it has a published, tabulated infrared spectrum of isolated molecules. Its identity was checked: our T1 structure has the same InChIKey as ChEMBL CHEMBL185 and PubChem CID 3385.

Why only the T1 form?

T1 (2,4-diketo) is the form of the clinical drug and the only form observed in the argon-matrix experiment; other forms were below detection there. A clean comparison needs the same form on both sides.

Why bent starts?

Screening pipelines hand over rough shapes from fast tools. We made strained starts on purpose (3.1 and 6.4 eV above the final energy) to mimic that, and asked whether every start reaches the same structure.

Why an argon-matrix spectrum?

No gas-phase infrared spectrum of 5-FU was found. Molecules isolated in solid argon at 6 K are the closest available match to a single molecule. Solid-state (KBr) spectra are shifted by hydrogen bonding and are not a fair check.

Why Module 2 starts from Run 2

The vibrations were computed once, from the structure that Module 1 relaxed from the 3.1 eV start. That keeps the infrared result tied to a bent start. No further runs were made after that, so there is no infrared from the 6.4 eV start.

Why report the scale factor the way we do

One factor (0.9736) was fitted on the same 25 bands it is judged on. We say so, and we report the unscaled errors next to the scaled ones, so a reader can judge both.

Which energies

All energies are electronic energies at fixed nuclei, from one method at a time (Skala-1.1 with the stated basis). Free energies in thermo.json use the harmonic, ideal-gas, rigid-rotor model.

Why a guard around the AI agent

A language model can be wrong. The agent only picks settings from a menu, must cite measured evidence, and every change is bounded and can be rolled back. The run stays safe whatever the agent says.

Figure design rules

  • Say "consistent with", never "reproduces". No "faster".
  • Only T1. Infrared numbers only from the Run 2 (3.1 eV) start.
  • Convergence quoted as "within 0.0072 Å and 0.51 meV".
  • Cost labelled as "gradient evaluations" (23, 64, 72), not "steps".
  • Scale factor printed as 0.9736, the unrounded fit, and stick positions use the same value.
  • Skala highlighted; no red for results that are not bad.
  • The screening workflow strip is context only and is marked as such.
